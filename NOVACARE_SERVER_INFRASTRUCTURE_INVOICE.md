## 2. What Novacare Should Expect from the New Server Upgrade

This dedicated production cloud upgrade transitions Novacare from shared sandbox constraints to a commercial-grade, high-availability operational backbone:

### ⚡ 1. 24/7 Continuous Availability (No Project Sleep)
* **Previous Limitation:** Shared/free cloud clusters enter sleep mode after inactivity, creating 15–30 second loading stalls during early-morning dispatch or weekend orders.
* **New Server Experience:** The production instance is permanently warm and active with **99.95% operational uptime**. Orders, inventory lookups, and customer queries load in under **150 milliseconds**.

### 📸 2. 100 GB Storage for Doorstep Proof of Delivery (POD)
* **What This Gives You:** Every delivery attempt now captures:
  1. High-resolution doorstep photographic proof.
  2. Customer digital touchscreen signature.
  3. Geotagged GPS coordinates (±1.5m precision).
* Novacare management can view, download, or audit photo delivery proofs directly from the Client Portal at any time for dispute resolution.

### 🔒 3. Automated Daily Backups & Financial Safety
* **Zero Data Loss Guarantee:** Automated snapshots are taken every 24 hours and stored for 7 days.
* Novacare's order manifests, sales history, customer records, and telesales closer commissions are completely protected against accidental loss or system failures.

### 💳 4. Instant Paystack Doorstep Settlement & Auto-Clearance
* **Automated Reconciliation:** When customers pay via Paystack (Debit Card, Transfer, USSD), payment confirms in under **2 seconds**.
* Cash liability is automatically deducted from the rider's custody, and funds settle directly into Novacare’s account profile without manual cash-handling risks.

### 👥 5. Dedicated Closer Portal & Multi-Hub Circuit Dispatch
